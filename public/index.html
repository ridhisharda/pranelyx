    Try gentle humming breath

    Inspired by Bhramari pranayama. Use it as a short relaxation exercise.

    1. Sit comfortably and let your shoulders drop.
    2. Breathe in gently through your nose if comfortable.
    3. Hum softly as you breathe out. Try three easy breaths, then breathe normally.

    Keep your breath comfortable and avoid holding it. Stop if you feel dizzy or short of breath.

    Check-ins stay in this browser. If you have trouble breathing, chest pain, or feel severely unwell, seek urgent medical help. For symptoms that worsen or persist, contact a healthcare professional.
